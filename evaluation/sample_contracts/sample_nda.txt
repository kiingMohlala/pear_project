MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date
by and between Acme Corp ("Disclosing Party") and Beta LLC ("Receiving Party").

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information means all non-public information, whether technical or business,
disclosed by either party, including trade secrets, customer lists, pricing, and product plans.

2. OBLIGATIONS
The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not
disclose it to any third party without prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.
The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

3. NON-COMPETE
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years thereafter, the Receiving
Party shall not engage in any business that competes with the Disclosing Party in the territory
of South Africa.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All intellectual property developed using Confidential Information shall be work for hire and
shall be owned exclusively by the Disclosing Party. The Receiving Party hereby assigns all
such rights irrevocably.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from any and all
claims arising out of unauthorized disclosure, including unlimited liability for consequential
damages.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in effect for five (5) years and shall automatically renew for
successive one-year periods unless terminated by either party with thirty (30) days written notice.
Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination perpetually.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Johannesburg.

8. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party,
except that the Disclosing Party may assign freely to any affiliate.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement.
