MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date
by and between Acme Corp ("Disclosing Party") and Beta LLC ("Receiving Party").

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information means all non-public information, whether technical or business,
disclosed by either party, including trade secrets, customer lists, pricing, product plans,
and source code.

2. OBLIGATIONS
The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not
disclose it to any third party without prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.
The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.
Confidential Information may be shared with employees under written NDAs.

3. NON-COMPETE
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of one (1) year thereafter, the Receiving
Party shall not engage in any business that competes with the Disclosing Party in the territory
of South Africa.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All intellectual property developed using Confidential Information shall be work for hire and
shall be owned exclusively by the Disclosing Party.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party from claims arising out of unauthorized
disclosure, limited to direct damages only (no consequential damages).

6. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in effect for three (3) years. Either party may terminate with
thirty (30) days written notice. Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
Any dispute shall first attempt mediation, then binding arbitration in Johannesburg.

8. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

9. RETURN OF MATERIALS
Upon termination, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information
within fifteen (15) days and certify destruction in writing.
